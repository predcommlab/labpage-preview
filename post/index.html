New lab at the RUB in Bochum

We started our new lab at the RUB in Bochum. Would you be interested in working here? The core infrastructure of THINK (for human research) consists of a 3T human MRI, a High-Performance Computing Cluster, multiple laboratories, amongst others for electrophysiological and behavioral studies, EEG, and virtual reality. All current positions are filled, but if you’re interested in a Postdoc or PhD on how expectations and context shape perception, and are considering applying for your own funding, I’d be very happy to hear from you!
