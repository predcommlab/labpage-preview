Join the lab

Postdoc and PhD positions in Cognitive Neuroscience on Predictive Cognition. Do you want to join us? Our research focuses on identifying the neural mechanisms that integrate sensory signals and prior expectations in cognition. We aim to understand how humans adapt to changing contexts, learn from experiences, and generalize prior knowledge to new sensory inputs under uncertainty. To explore these questions, we combine computational modeling using deep neural networks and large language models with behavioral and neuroimaging techniques, including eye tracking, pupillometry, EEG, and structural and functional MRI (s/fMRI). Predictive processing, particularly predictive coding, offers a potential framework for understanding adaptive behavior. By testing these theories against alternative models in areas like learning, perception, and memory, we aim to uncover the neuro-computational mechanisms underlying human cognition, such as in language, speech, and face perception. Informal inquiries are very welcome.

The positions will be based at THINK, the Center for Theoretical and Integrative Neuroscience and Cognitive Science at the Ruhr University in Bochum, Germany.

What we offer

Hands-on supervision

Learn various data acquisition and analysis techniques — e.g., representational similarity analysis (RSA).

Research environment

A dynamic, interdisciplinary, and international research environment.

Infrastructure

A research-dedicated 3T MR scanner, eye-tracking and EEG laboratories, excellent infrastructure for behavioural testing — plus access to a 7T MR scanner.

Training

Multiple training opportunities within the institute and the university.

Requirements

PhD position

  • A Master of Science or an equivalent degree in neuroscience, cognitive science, psychology, or a related discipline
  • Experience in programming (e.g., Python, MATLAB)
  • Keen interest in experimental approaches to study predictive processing, including magnetic resonance imaging (MRI)
  • Motivation to work independently as well as in a team environment
  • Strong organizational skills
  • Fluency in English

Postdoc position

Everything listed for the PhD position, plus:

  • A PhD in neuroscience, cognitive science, psychology, or a related discipline
